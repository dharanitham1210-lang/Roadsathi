# Slide 2 – Problem Statement

## Problem

- Highway breakdowns become safety emergencies, especially at night or in isolated areas.
- Finding reliable roadside assistance is difficult.
- Travellers depend on scattered phone numbers and local contacts.
- Uncertain pricing can lead to unexpected repair costs during emergencies.
- Nearby garages remain disconnected.
- Stranded travellers struggle to find help.
- Poor network connectivity can make internet-dependent assistance unavailable.
- Existing roadside assistance often focuses on the vehicle, leaving the safety and travel needs of the family unresolved.

## Proposed Solution – RoadSathi

RoadSathi is an integrated highway rescue platform that connects stranded travellers, verified mechanics, parts suppliers and family members through one emergency workflow.

### Key Functions

- Connects travellers to the nearest mechanic in 5–10 minutes.
- Provides one-tap SOS.
- Supports plain SMS when there is no data.
- Provides price fixation before the mechanic leaves.
- Helps the family get home safely when the vehicle cannot be fixed.
- Connects travellers with idle nearby garages.
- Uses AI fault triage to identify possible faults.
- Helps mechanics bring the right tools and parts.
- Supports location-based rescue requests through SMS.
- Uses a 4-digit arrival code for verification.
- Allows family members to monitor the trip.
