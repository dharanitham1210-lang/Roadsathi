# Slide 5 – Impact and Benefits

## Traveller Safety

- Faster emergency response through SOS + GPS.
- Verified mechanics with KYC and arrival-code.
- Family tracking through Guardian Link.
- Reduces prolonged roadside exposure.

## Faster & Efficient Rescue

- AI fault triage reduces diagnosis time.
- Parts-aware routing reduces unnecessary trips.
- Connects travellers with nearby available mechanics.
- Target: 5–10 minutes to first mechanic.

## Economic & Local Impact

- Activates idle local garages.
- Creates additional earning opportunities for mechanics.
- Reduces unnecessary travel and repair delays.
- Supports local spare-parts businesses.

## Long-Term Impact

- Builds a connected highway rescue ecosystem.
- Can generate highway breakdown/blackspot insights.
- Supports expansion to more highway corridors.
- Potential integration with fleets, authorities and insurers.

## Vision

"FROM A STRANDED VEHICLE TO A COORDINATED, SAFER JOURNEY HOME."
